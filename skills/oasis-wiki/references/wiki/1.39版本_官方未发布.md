- `UGCWidgetManagerSystem.CreateWidget` / `ShowWidget` / `HideWidget` / `AddToSlot` → `UGCUIManagerSystem`
- `UGCGameSystem.SpawnActor` → `UGCObjectUtility` 或工程内既有生成封装
